# LPC2220

Siehe auch [LPC2220_Pin-Belegung](LPC2220%20Pin-Belegung.md "LPC2220 Pin-Belegung")

ARM7TDMI without MMU

- ARM v4T architecture
  - ARMcode and Thumbcode (16-bit subset of ARMcode)

<!-- -->

- JTAG
- EmbeddedICE-Realtime Debug Unit
- Embedded Trace Macrocell

<!-- -->

- On-Chip-Serial Bootloader on ROM

[ARM Architecture Reference Manual](http://rtds.cs.tamu.edu/web_462/techdocs/ARM/instruct/DDI0100E_ARM_ARM.pdf)

Allocation:

- BOOTP1:0 (P2.27:P2.26)
  - Value P2.27=1 & P2.26=0 - Interpretation 16 Bit Bootflash on CS0

<!-- -->

- BANK-Chipselect
  - CS0 Flash0 16-bit first flashrom from cpu counted
  - CS1 LCDisplay 8-bit A0 on CPU=R/S on Display
  - CS2 Flash1 16-bit second flashrom

<!-- -->

- Clocksource
  - External 10 MHz Crystal
